About Woody

Woody Booth is a hand-restored vintage trailer turned mobile photo booth — built for weddings, corporate events, birthdays, and reunions alike. Prefer an indoor setup? Ask about our Cedar Chevron Woody Wall.

Props Included

Every booking comes with a curated prop collection matched to your event's theme, included at no extra cost.

Instant Prints

Every session prints two matching 2×6 photo strips on the spot, with unlimited sessions throughout your event.

Fully Tailored

Custom screens, logo branding, themed props, and backdrops — every detail is tailored to your event.
